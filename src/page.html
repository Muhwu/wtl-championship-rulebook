0.0Introduction

The following rules are for the WTL Championship 2026 associated with We Teach League. By participating in the league, players are acknowledging that they have read and understand the rules in their entirety.

0.1Out of Game Behavior

0.1.1

Any harassment or personal attacks or any kind will result in penalties for the player(s) in question depending on the severity of the acts. This includes but is not limited to: bullying, racism, personal insults, and offensive language.

0.1.2

Disrespectfulness of any kind will not be tolerated).

0.1.3

Not safe for work content of any kind has no place in the discord.

0.1.4

Arguing with staff decisions will result in a warning. Inquiring about interpretation or implementation of a rule is allowed, as long as it is not in regards to a punishment that was handed down.

0.1.5

Team names, team logos, and player names must be appropriate. Players will be asked by admins to change a team name, a team logo, or player names if they are deemed inappropriate.

0.2In Game Behavior

0.2.1

Emotes that can be muted in-game (e.g., emote wheel, mastery flash, situational emotes for first blood) are allowed to be used. Dancing and laughing are also allowed. If bothered by these emotes, players can mute them through their settings (dancing and laughing are muted by muting chat).

0.2.2

All chat should only be used in a sportsmanlike manner and when warranted. E.g., “ggwp,” “glhf”. Mean-spirited all chat usage may result in game suspensions for the offending player(s).

0.2.3

If any situations come up during game time (eligibility questions, rule violations, unsportsmanlike behavior in the lobby, etc.), do not play the game. Captains should ping one of the officials on the discord server.

0.2.4

Whenever a new or reworked champion is released, they will not be available in league matches until one (1) patch after the champion is released.

0.2.5

In order to participate in an official match for a team, players must be in the discord server and be properly registered on that team. The onus is on the opposing captain to check that the players in their lobby are registered on the approved roster lists.

0.2.6

The matches will take place every Friday at 8pm (EST for NA and CET for EU). The captains are free to schedule their matches at another time, but it must be approved by both captains. Teams must be willing to field substitute players if need be.

0.2.7

Matches must be played five (5) vs. five (5). If the initial five (5) players for each team are not present at the scheduled match time, see the table below:

Time late to matchPunishment
5 minutesLoss of 1st ban
10 minutesLoss of 1st two bans
20 minutesLoss of 1st game
30 minutesLoss of whole match/series

Please note that captains are encouraged to enforce these punishments to incentivise respect and sportsmanship, but not obligated to do so.

1.1.1

1.1.2

Players must report all accounts that are level 30 or higher. If it is discovered that a player does not disclose all of their accounts, the player, their captain, and their team will be punished.

2.0Divisions

2.1Accepted Teams

2.1.1

All teams must consist of at least 6 players. Teams may have a maximum of 10 players.

2.1.2

The league will accept at most 8 total teams.

2.2Evaluating Teams’ Point Evaluations

2.2.1

All accepted players will be assigned a point value based on the stipulations outlined in [2.2.2]. A team’s point evaluation (TPE) is determined by adding the assigned point values of the 5 starters on the roster, known for the rest of this document as TPE players. The substitute players may not be at a higher evaluation from the starter.

2.2.3

A player cannot change his role at any point post-registration. However, a player can be registered twice in a team (starter Top, substitute ADC for example) and will receive 2 SLOPE evaluations.

2.3.3

3 out of the 5 starting players of each team must be a member of the WTL academies. The remaining 2 may be non-members. This rule will keep being applied for the full duration of the event.

3.0Roster Changes

3.1Mid-Match Substitutions and Roster Announcements

3.1.1

Substitute players already on a team’s roster are allowed to play in any given game.

3.1.2

Captains should confirm what their roster will be at least 24 hours in advance and post it in the roster announcement channel.

3.1.2.1

If a team plans on making substitutions during a match, the sub needs to be on the announced roster and it must be announced in which games they will be playing in the announcement mentioned in [3.1.2].

3.1.2.2

The announced roster must be between 5 and 7 players.

3.1.2.3

If any last minute change must be made, the team may change it, but must give 10 additional minutes to the enemy team to prepare and will lose two bans during the first ban phase for all games in which the roster isn’t what was announced 24 hours before the game.

3.2Other Roster Stipulations

3.2.1

If a player is removed from a team for rule violations or any other reason, that player’s team will have 1 week from the league’s announcement of the ruling to fill that player's spot on that team’s roster. The new player must be of equal of lower point value.

3.2.2

An individual player is only allowed to be registered on 1 roster at a time as a starter and may be a sub in another team from another division.

4.0Pauses, Disconnects, Remakes, Forfeits

4.1Pauses and Breaks

4.1.1

If a player disconnects, or if any serious issue occurs, the game can be paused for up to ten (10) minutes by each team per individual game. If the pause surpasses ten (10) total minutes of pause time from a singular team, the game must unpause and be played out or the offending team must forfeit.

4.1.2

Teams may not pause during a fight or right before a fight will begin. Teams may not pause in the middle of combat. Both captains should confirm they are ready before the game is unpaused.

4.1.2.1

A “fight” is defined as a sequence in the game where at least 2 players are in active combat with one another. The phrase “before a fight” refers to an instance where it is clear that a fight will break out within a few seconds (ex: A jungler starting a gank or a scuttle crab contest).

4.1.3

For games after the first game of a series, all players should be in the lobby and the draft should start no later than fifteen (15) minutes following the conclusion of the previous game. Games should start no more than five (5) minutes after the draft finishes.

4.2Disconnects

If teams run into unfixable problems, such as a power or internet outage, the captain should contact both the other captain and the admin team. If the issue is deemed fixable, the game will not be remade or postponed. The /pause function is approved for use during these situations. If the other captain is in agreement, teams may surpass their originally allotted ten (10) minutes of pause time.

4.3Remakes

4.3.1

Unless both parties are experiencing massive server lag and admins deem the game unplayable – remakes will not be given. Natural disasters or life-threatening situations are the only other instances where a remake may be considered. Decisions will be made by the admin team once they understand the situation.

4.3.2

Remakes are not permitted if a player incorrectly selects their runes or summoner spells.

5.0Format of Play

5.1Voice Channels

5.1.1

All registered players must be in the Discord server with the proper naming convention.

5.1.2

Teams must play in their designated Discord voice channel for all matches. The voice channels are locked to just players.

5.2Format of Games

5.2.1

All matches should be played in custom lobbies with the abbreviations as the name and “WTL123” as the password. (ex: SKY vs SBD, pw:ACL123)

5.2.2

All matches are played in a best-of-3 format, except for finals, which will be a best-of-5.

5.2.4

All the lobbies must be open for any player, coach, manager or team owner that wishes to spectate the match.

5.2.5

Any member of the league may stream and cast the game on twitch, but must give their twitch link in the appropriate channel in the discord server.

5.3Draft

5.3.1

All matches will use the “Fearless” draft format and must be done in Drafter.lol.

5.3.1.1

After the draft is completed, each team must specify the role of each champion during the role confirmation phase (in Drafter.lol).

5.3.2

In the game lobby, all players must line up in the standard order that follows the roles they intend to play before starting the draft.

  1. Top
  2. Jungle
  3. Mid
  4. Bottom
  5. Support

5.4Side Selection

5.4.1

Side selection is chosen through a coin flip. Both captains shall go in the coin flip channel where one captain will flip a coin on this website or simply on google. The captain that won the coin flip can choose between Red or Blue side. All games after the first are the loser's choice: the team that lost the most recent game has side selection in the next game.

5.4.2

Playoff side selection is determined by seeding. The higher seed has selection for the first game of a series throughout the duration of the playoffs.

5.4.3

In the playoffs, the team with side selection of the first game must communicate their decision at least twenty four (24) hours prior to the start of the match with their roster. If this team does not decide on a side by this time, the side selection will be decided like in regular season with a coin flip.

5.5Schedules

5.5.1

Regular season schedules will have a set number of weeks in which teams have one scheduled match per week. These matches must be played within the week they are assigned. Weekly matchups are distributed prior to the season starting.

5.6Tie-breaking Format

5.7.1

In the case of a points tie between teams at the conclusion of the regular season, the first tie-breaker is the total win rate of all games played during the regular season.

5.7.2

If the teams have an identical win rate, the next tie breaker is head-to-head results.

5.7.3

If teams are still tied, the tied teams will play a one game tie-breaker to determine their place in the standings.

Section B

SLOPE Evaluations

The Standardized Level Of Play Estimate, by WD Maverick. One formula that applies to everyone and gives a fair picture of your level of play in a competitive setting.

Variables

x
Current rank, at the time the application is submitted
y
Peak rank in 2026 (used with 200+ games in 2026)
y′
Peak rank across 2025 and 2026 (used with fewer than 200 games)
z
Ranked solo/duo games played in 2026
a
OTP status: share of your games on your main champion
b
Role status: 0 on-role, ½ secondary role, 1 off-role

How it reads

Only ranked solo/duo games count. Ranks use the apex-tier scale with Master 0 LP as the zero point, so Diamond 4 0 LP is −400 LP, Emerald 4 0 LP is −800 LP, and so on. Apex players who decayed recently use their rank from before the decay.

Your current rank is the base. It is pulled toward your peak by a weight of 150/z + ¼, so the more games you play, the more the result leans toward your current rank. At 600 games the result sits halfway between peak and current. The peak never fully disappears: the weight never drops below a quarter.

The penalty terms account for one-trick players and players off their main role. q scales from 0 at 50% on one champion to 1 at 100%; below 50% there is no OTP adjustment. The qb term in Division 1 covers players who are both one-tricks and off-role.

With fewer than 200 games it is hard to estimate your level, so your SLOPE equals your highest peak of the last two seasons.
